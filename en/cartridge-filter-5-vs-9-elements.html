5 vs 9-element industrial cartridge filter housing

Engineering selection does not start with element count. Define the product, line capacity and filtration duty first; then evaluate count, length, rating and cleaning conditions together.

Published: 24 October 20268-minute readB2B procurement guide
In this guideShort answerThe buying problemVerified dataSelection methodRFQ checklistFAQ
Short answer: A 9-element housing is not automatically the better choice or a guarantee of higher capacity than a 5-element housing. Behriz Mobadel publishes 5- and 9-cartridge arrangements, 12-, 18- and 24-inch housing lengths, and 2-, 5- and 10-micron ratings. The final combination must be confirmed against the actual fluid, line capacity, pressure drop and cleaning conditions.

Problem: why “9-element housing” is not a complete specification

Element count is often used as shorthand for capacity in enquiries. It is not sufficient for fabrication or quotation comparison. Two housings with the same count may use different cartridge lengths, media, ratings, fluid conditions and pressure-drop design bases.

Buyers should avoid comparing prices for a “9-element housing” until the duty and operating basis are aligned across quotations.

The capacity range applies to the product family. Official data does not assign a fixed portion to either arrangement, so this guide does not create such a mapping.

Solution: close the selection in five steps

  1. Define the product: water, juice, syrup, malt beverage or another fluid, including temperature and solids condition.
  2. State capacity precisely: normal and maximum flow, operating hours, and continuous or batch duty.
  3. Define filter duty: RO pretreatment, final filtration or downstream-equipment protection.
  4. Make the rating testable: state whether the micron value is nominal or absolute.
  5. Agree pressure-drop and service bases: clean differential pressure, action point, cleaning method and service criteria.
Decision rule: Freeze operating conditions and filtration target first. Then compare proposals by effective media area, cartridge length, pressure drop and service plan. Element count is only one design input.

Cartridge-filter housing RFQ checklist

  • Product and every fluid passing through the equipment
  • Line capacity and daily operating hours
  • Available inlet temperature and pressure
  • Incoming solids condition and outlet target
  • Required rating and nominal/absolute basis
  • Cleaning method and permitted downtime
  • Footprint, service height and connection points
  • Instrumentation, controls, installation and commissioning scope

If media type, allowable differential pressure or cleaning cycle is unknown, mark it “engineering confirmation required” rather than inserting an assumed number.

Frequently asked questions

Is a 5-element or 9-element housing better?

Neither is universally better. Product, flow, cartridge type and length, rating, solids load, pressure drop and service plan must be assessed together.

What is the published Behriz capacity range?

The product-family range is 10,000–35,000 L/h. Final capacity requires project confirmation.

Which housing lengths are available?

The published lengths are 12, 18 and 24 inches. The final combination is defined in the proposal.

Does nine cartridges always mean more flow?

That cannot be concluded from count alone. Media, length, fluid conditions, rating and pressure drop also govern selection.

What is required for a quotation?

Provide the product, line capacity, temperature, pressure, target rating, solids condition, operating hours and cleaning conditions.

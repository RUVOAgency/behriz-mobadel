Diatomite dosing pump for Kieselguhr filtration: selection and RFQ guide

Do not select the pump from filtration-line flow alone. Actual dosing demand, slurry behaviour, injection pressure, control range and acceptance testing must be defined before requesting a price.

Short answer: The Behriz Mobadel diaphragm dosing pump is listed for controlled diatomite injection in filtration systems and precision juice and malt-beverage applications. Published capacities are 135 and 600 L/h. These are pump capacities—not filtration-line capacity or dry-media consumption. Final selection requires an approved recipe, slurry concentration and behaviour, minimum/maximum flow, injection pressure, control method and acceptance criteria.

Problem: why filtration-line flow does not select the dosing pump

A filtration line may require different injection rates at different stages or for different products. Product flow, filter type and media name are starting inputs; pump duty comes from the required liquid or slurry dosing range and its operating conditions.

An RFQ that states only “20,000 L/h line” leaves slurry concentration, injection pressure, minimum stable flow, mixing, settling, control and cleaning undefined. Suppliers may then quote the same nominal capacity against different assumptions.

Neither 135 nor 600 L/h should be interpreted as dry-media dose, filter capacity or guaranteed output at every pressure. Slurry concentration, discharge pressure, density, settling and pipework affect the operating point and belong in the technical proposal.

Solution: close selection in five stages

  1. Define the dosing duty. State filter, product, process stage and dosing recipe or scenario.
  2. Provide a flow range. Record minimum, normal and maximum slurry flow for every product and operating mode.
  3. Describe the slurry. State media, concentration, density, temperature, settling behaviour, particles and mixing method.
  4. Close hydraulics and control. Define injection pressure, elevation, line length, command signal, feedback and filter interlocks.
  5. Make acceptance measurable. Agree calibration method, test flows, duration, repeats, error limit and FAT/SAT conditions.
Technical boundary: No public official value is confirmed here for operating pressure, wetted materials, motor power, accuracy, repeatability, turndown, allowable slurry concentration or diatomite consumption. These require project engineering and approval.

Diatomite dosing-pump URS checklist

  • Product type and normal/maximum filtration-line flow
  • Filter type and model
  • Process stage and injection point on the P&ID
  • Dosing recipe and its basis; mark “trial required” when unknown
  • Media and approved project grade, plus slurry concentration
  • Density, temperature, particles and settling behaviour
  • Minimum, normal and maximum required pump flow
  • Line pressure, elevation, injection-pipe length and diameter
  • Tank volume, mixing and prevention of settling or dead zones
  • Manual/automatic control and command or feedback signal
  • Standby, bypass, calibration and drainage requirements
  • Cleaning, flushing and allowable downtime
  • FAT/SAT criteria, handover documents and spares

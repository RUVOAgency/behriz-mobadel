Progressing cavity pump dry-run protection: interlock and RFQ guide

A practical guide for technical, automation and procurement teams to turn “dry-run protection included” into testable signals, trip logic and acceptance evidence.

Published: 28 November 20268-minute readB2B problem-solving guide
In this guideShort answerProblemInsightSolutionRFQ checklistAcceptance testFAQSources and limits

Short answer: Do not close pump protection with one phrase or one sensor. Separate empty-vessel, feed loss, air ingress, blocked suction, closed-valve and high-discharge-pressure scenarios. For each, record detection signal, validated delay, shutdown action, alarm, reset and test method in the control narrative. No universal trip setpoint, allowable dry-run time or restart logic is published for the Behriz Mobadel pump; those values require project approval.

Problem: what does “dry-run protection included” mean?

It may describe a vessel level switch, flow proof, stator-temperature monitoring, PLC logic or a combination. If the RFQ names only the feature, quotations can differ materially in instrument count, fault coverage, trip behaviour and testability.

Dry running is not the same as overpressure. Feed loss and a blocked discharge are different hazards with different signals and actions; both belong in the pump protection review.

Verified published Behriz pump data

The official product page describes a progressing-cavity / positive-displacement pump with a published 1–25 t/h range and available sizes from 1¼ to 4 inches. Flow control can be assessed through motor speed and an inverter; final stator, seal, motor, gearbox, pressure and speed depend on the product and line conditions. These values are not protective setpoints, allowable dry-run time or a guarantee at every operating condition.

Solution: close protection in five documents

1. Cause & EffectList every cause, signal, alarm, trip and output state separately.
2. Control NarrativeDefine start permissives, delays, latching, reset and restart in production, draining and cleaning modes.
3. Instrument ListState device, range, connection, location, output and supply responsibility.
4. I/O ListRecord panel, PLC and upstream/downstream signals with fail-safe states.
5. FAT/SAT ProtocolAgree how every fault is simulated, the expected response, records and approver.

Use the Persian progressing-cavity pump URS and RFQ guide to define duty point, materials, cleaning and supply scope. Current published product data is on the Behriz pump page.

Dry-run protection RFQ checklist

  • Product type and normal/maximum line capacity
  • Minimum, normal and maximum pump flow
  • Temperature and viscosity during operation and startup
  • Feed vessel/equipment, minimum operating level and emptying behaviour
  • Suction arrangement, elevation, pipe length, valves and air-ingress risk
  • Discharge pressure and every blocked/closed-path condition
  • Production, draining, CIP, short-stop and emergency modes
  • Existing instruments and signals
  • Panel or PLC, signal types and inverter interface
  • Alarm, trip, reset and restart philosophy
  • FAT/SAT method and required handover evidence
  • Included, optional and excluded supply items

Technical boundary: Sensor selection and location, temperature/level/pressure setpoints, trip delay, allowable no-feed duration, restart logic and safety-performance class are not defined in the public official Behriz data. No universal number or guarantee is published here.

Frequently asked questions

Can a progressing cavity pump run dry?

No general allowable time is published for the Behriz pump; do not base protection on a guessed time.

Which sensor prevents dry running?

Level, flow proof and stator temperature are options to assess, but selection and setpoints are project-specific.

Is a vessel low-level switch sufficient?

Not always; inventory in the vessel does not necessarily prove actual flow through the pump.

May the pump restart automatically after a trip?

Only under the approved control narrative, with trip cause, flow path, feed and reset permission addressed.

What belongs in the protection RFQ?

Send product, line capacity, flow, temperature, viscosity, feed source, suction/discharge, operating modes, instruments, controls and test criteria.

Syrup deaerator: URS, RFQ and procurement guide

Line capacity alone is not a comparable specification. Product properties, real flow, process conditions, cleaning and outlet acceptance must be defined before requesting a price.

Published: 14 November 20268-minute readB2B procurement guide
In this guideShort answerProcurement problemVerified dataSelection methodURS checklistBid comparisonFAQ
Short answer: A concentrated-syrup deaerator is assessed for deoxygenating syrup in beverage and food-process lines. The published Behriz Mobadel capacity range is 2,000–5,000 L/h. Final selection and performance acceptance require syrup type, concentration or Brix, temperature, normal and maximum flow, foaming behaviour, cleaning programme and a defined outlet measurement method.

Problem: why “a 5,000-litre deaerator” is not a complete RFQ

Nameplate flow is only one input. Two syrups at the same flow can differ in concentration, temperature, foaming, operating schedule and cleaning requirements. If these are absent, suppliers may price different assumptions and the bids are not technically comparable.

Do not transfer the specifications of a cold-water deaerator to syrup service. The product family, published range and design inputs are different, so the project needs a separate datasheet.

The published range does not guarantee the same result for every syrup. Viscosity, Brix, residence time, foaming, product loss, utility consumption and outlet oxygen are acceptance claims only when their conditions and measurement method are written into the contract.

Solution: move from product problem to acceptance criterion

  1. Define the feed. Record syrup, available formula, Brix or concentration, temperature, possible solids and product variations.
  2. State real capacity. Separate minimum, normal and maximum flow, batch volume, hours and allowable stops.
  3. Locate the equipment. Identify process stage, upstream/downstream equipment, available pressures and footprint limits.
  4. Include cleaning. Define cleaning chemicals, temperature, time, CIP path, drainage and readiness for the next batch.
  5. Make acceptance measurable. Agree outlet target, instrument, method, sample points, repeat count and approval responsibility before order.
Technical boundary: This page states no assumed vacuum, operating temperature, allowable viscosity, outlet oxygen, residence time or energy use. These values require project testing, engineering and approval.

Concentrated-syrup deaerator URS checklist

  • Product name, available formula and all syrups sharing the line
  • Brix or concentration, density and viscosity at actual process temperature
  • Minimum, normal and maximum flow, or batch volume and frequency
  • Inlet temperature and allowable product-temperature range
  • Inlet dissolved oxygen and outlet target with proposed method
  • Foaming behaviour and product-loss or carry-over limit
  • Upstream/downstream pressure and line connection point
  • Available power, water, air, vacuum and drain conditions
  • Cleaning method, CIP chemistry and allowable downtime
  • Automation, data recording, alarms and line interface
  • Footprint, nozzle orientation, service access and supply boundary
  • FAT/SAT criteria, test repeats and sampling responsibility

Frequently asked questions

What is a concentrated-syrup deaerator?

It is equipment for deoxygenating concentrated syrup in beverage and food-process lines; final selection depends on actual product and line data.

What is the published capacity range?

The published Behriz Mobadel range is 2,000–5,000 L/h. Final project capacity requires engineering confirmation.

Can cold-water deaerator data be used for syrup?

No. Product, concentration, temperature, flow, cleaning and outlet criteria must be specified separately.

What information is required for a quotation?

Send syrup type, line flow or batch, concentration, temperature, foaming, cleaning, utilities and acceptance criteria.

What outlet dissolved-oxygen value is guaranteed?

No general value is confirmed for this model; target, measurement method and test conditions belong in the project datasheet.

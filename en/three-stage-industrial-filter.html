“Three-stage” is not a complete purchasing specification. A proposal should define the duty of each stage, its operating basis, and how the outlet will be accepted.

Short answer: Behriz Mobadel publishes the three-stage filter for food-process oil, extract and syrup, with a 2,000–20,000 L/h range, Stainless Steel 304L–316L construction, and independent draining and cleaning at each stage. The function, media and acceptance criterion of every stage must be defined for the actual project; they cannot be inferred from the words “three-stage.”

Problem: why “three-stage filter” is not enough for an order

Search results often confuse an industrial three-stage system with household water-filter packs. This page concerns an industrial food-process filtration system, not a set of domestic water cartridges.

In an industrial RFQ, stage count still does not define the process. A request that says only “three-stage filter for syrup” can produce proposals with different configurations and supply boundaries, making price comparisons unreliable.

Solution: turn each stage into a deliverable question

  1. Stage duty: what problem it addresses and how it connects to the next stage.
  2. Proposed components: equipment, media and product-contact parts confirmed for the project.
  3. Inlet and outlet basis: flow, temperature, pressure and the required quality indicator.
  4. Cleaning and drainage: path, sequence, chemistry and allowable downtime.
  5. Acceptance: sampling method, measured indicator and responsible party.
Technical boundary: the official public data does not assign a fixed medium or performance claim to stage one, two or three. These must appear in the approved project datasheet and proposal.

How should quotations be compared?

No fixed price is published. Compare amounts only after confirming that every proposal uses the same product, capacity basis and outlet requirement.

  • State the grade of product-contact materials separately.
  • Define the function and components of all three stages.
  • Separate included and excluded supply items.
  • Document cleaning, consumables and replacement parts.
  • Agree test conditions and acceptance before ordering.
